# Homepage Product Showcase and Motion Design

## Goal

Turn the homepage into a richer outdoor-apparel catalog experience while preserving its premium restrained visual language.

## Approved experience

- Show all four real catalog categories: Fleece Jackets, Outdoor Pants, Cargo Shorts, and Knitwear.
- Add a Featured Outdoor Collection with eight real products, four visible on desktop and horizontally scrollable on smaller screens.
- Use outdoor-technical motion: staggered reveal, restrained image depth, seam-like hover tracing, and distinct soft/decisive entrances by garment type.
- Animate Manufacturing Excellence as a connected three-stage process.
- Replace the current product-detail collage with a clean garment quality-inspection scene.
- Disable nonessential hover motion on touch layouts and honor `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Content and visual constraints

- Use only real product names, models, links, and product imagery already associated with this tenant.
- No invented certification, facility promise, watermark, embedded text, or unsupported marketing claim.
- Keep readable foreground/background contrast in all static and animated states.
- Motion must not delay navigation or obscure content.
